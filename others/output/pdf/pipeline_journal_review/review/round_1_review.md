# Independent journal review — round 1

**Manuscript:** Two-View Image-Based Anthropometry: Profile-Guided Reconstruction and Geometric Measurement  
**Version:** `review/submitted_v0.tex` (full manuscript reviewed).  
**Recommendation:** **NOT SATISFIED / REITERATE. Not ready for journal submission.**

## Scope and standard

This review assesses a general scientific methods paper, not a named journal's formal checklist. I read the complete submitted TeX, its bibliography, the earlier editorial audit and source manifest, and relevant historical fitting/uncertainty implementation. This is a methodological review, not independent replication of the human pilot. The separate sizing recommender and mathematical note are outside scope.

The correct completed-square conic recovery, weighted similarity equations, perimeter derivatives and the small-curvature expansion are internally reasonable under their stated assumptions. The paper is candid about weaknesses. The present rejection recommendation follows from unresolved scientific evidence and implementation consistency, not from the presence of candid language.

In particular, the quoted sentence about a sign discrepancy is a disclosure of R01. Removing that sentence without correcting and rerunning the estimator would conceal the problem. Historical pilot outputs, synthetic algebra checks and independent physical validation are different kinds of evidence.

## Acceptance contract

**Overall PASS** means the blocking and major issues below have evidence-based resolution appropriate to the claims retained in the paper. The reviewer will apply these same criteria on re-review. Correctly narrowed claims can make a specific issue inapplicable, but a methods proposal with no adequate demonstration of scientific utility will not receive an overall PASS as an evaluated journal method.

**Editorial/mathematical consistency** is a separate assessment: the paper can become a clear, honest proposal and have a computationally verified reference formulation while still lacking independent human validation, novelty evidence or participant governance. If those items remain, overall verdict stays **NOT SATISFIED**. This process cannot guarantee a journal editor's acceptance.

No corrected human values, consent, ethical clearance, experiments, timing or novelty evidence may be invented. Synthetic tests must be explicitly identified as synthetic and cannot be substituted for human agreement evidence. Changes should preserve an audit trail and distinguish revised research reference code from the historical application.

## Prioritized findings

### R01 — Corrected method and historical numerical evidence refer to different estimators [BLOCKING]

**Location:** Abstract; Section 4.1, Eq. (conic recovery); Table 1; Conclusion.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Verification identifies a sign discrepancy in conic-axis recovery; the reported pilot values precede its correction.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The completed-square equation is correct, but Table 1 and headline MAE were generated by a demonstrably different axis-recovery expression. A known-ellipse test proves a real numerical discrepancy. Disclosure is necessary, yet does not validate the described corrected pipeline; deleting the disclosure would worsen the defect.

**Resolution criterion:** Provide an isolated corrected implementation for both measurement and sensitivity recovery, regression tests, and traceable end-to-end reruns for all reported inputs. Replace the primary results with corrected outputs and document numerical changes. Alternatively remove historical values as evidence of the corrected method, retain their provenance in the review archive, and explicitly recast the paper as an unvalidated specification; that alternative resolves misleading presentation but does not close R02.

**Evidence required:** Versioned patch, known-ellipse tests including nonuniform sampling and translated/rotated cases, input/model hashes, complete per-case corrected output and logs.  
**Work required:** math, code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R02 — No independent evaluation of corrected human anthropometry [BLOCKING]

**Location:** Section 6, Historical Pilot Results and Limitations; Conclusion.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The pilot comprises three adult male project authors

**Why this damages acceptance:** Three developer-participants cannot establish generalization, clinical/garment usefulness, or robustness. The paper has no independently collected corrected evaluation and does not substantiate an experimental methods contribution. Synthetic verification can check algebra and chosen shape models but cannot establish physical anthropometric validity.

**Resolution criterion:** Evaluate the corrected frozen method on independently acquired human observations with a justified sample design, repeated acquisition, specified population and endpoints, and uncertainty at participant level. If such evidence is unavailable, mark the paper as a methods proposal and leave journal readiness unresolved; a prose reframe alone earns no overall PASS.

**Evidence required:** Author-provided ethically acquired independent data, prespecified evaluation design, corrected per-subject results and analysis.  
**Work required:** data, code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R03 — The claimed adaptations have no causal ablation evidence [MAJOR]

**Location:** Introduction; Section 3; Section 6 final paragraph.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** These adaptations require ablation to establish an accuracy benefit

**Why this damages acceptance:** No results isolate robust profile filtering, column thickness limitation, waist displacement, or strong section opening. A plausible equation cannot show that a proposed adaptation improves measurement; morphology could dominate any observed change.

**Resolution criterion:** Run matched-input, fixed-perception ablations of conventional occupancy, filtered profile occupancy without thickness cap, full profile coupling, waist on/off, and section morphology settings. Report per-endpoint error and failure rate. Synthetic mechanism ablations can close a narrow geometric claim only, with human accuracy benefit remaining unproven under R02.

**Evidence required:** Executable configurations, common input manifest, per-case branch outputs and paired changes; human evidence for accuracy claims.  
**Work required:** code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R04 — Reference tape measurements may not measure the same quantities as the outputs [MAJOR]

**Location:** Sections 3.3, 4.2 and 6; Table 1.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** These prescribed levels do not search for maximum chest or hip girth.

**Why this damages acceptance:** Chest and hip use prescribed landmark-relative levels, waist uses an engineering fraction, and shoulder uses a transverse half-ellipse path. The reference protocol, endpoint markings, posture, tape tension, clothing, operator, and repeated readings are absent. Citing ISO does not establish that the pilot follows its definitions. A mismatch can dominate all numerical error.

**Resolution criterion:** Give an operational definition for every evaluated endpoint and the exact tape protocol at matched levels and endpoints. Separate projected shoulder breadth, model-based surface path, and hip-to-ankle proxy; exclude unmatched outcomes from accuracy aggregation. Do not retroactively claim an undocumented ISO protocol.

**Evidence required:** Contemporaneous reference records or new matched reference measurements; illustrated endpoint protocol and operator/repeat information.  
**Work required:** data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R05 — The five-term RSS has no demonstrated interpretation as physical uncertainty [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 5; Table 1 delta column; Abstract/results interpretation.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** This sum neglects covariance among terms derived from the same masks.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The localization spread is assumed rather than measured; the same mask affects several terms, stature is shared, and important acquisition/model errors are absent. Calling the result a sensitivity indicator is appropriately cautious, but its prominent plus/minus presentation invites an uncertainty interpretation unsupported by coverage evidence.

**Resolution criterion:** Either replace the RSS with a branch-consistent propagated model whose input spreads and covariances are justified and empirically checked, or remove it from the primary human comparison and label all remaining calculations explicitly as conditional sensitivity examples. State units, input assumptions and excluded errors. No confidence/coverage claim may be inferred.

**Evidence required:** Derivation with units and covariance structure, empirical localization/repeat-capture evidence if statistical uncertainty is claimed; otherwise narrowly scoped sensitivity tests.  
**Work required:** math, code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R06 — Unavailable sensitivity information is encoded as zero [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 5 conic-fit sensitivity paragraph.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Five-point fits or unavailable covariance return zero in the historical helper

**Why this damages acceptance:** Singular fits, absent covariance and failed perturbations can appear maximally certain. This is an implementation defect, even when the manuscript admits it; it invalidates the historical RSS as an operational uncertainty output.

**Resolution criterion:** Use an explicit unavailable status with reason, propagate missingness to combined outputs, and distinguish it from a successfully computed zero. Exercise five-point, rank-deficient, invalid-perturbation and genuine zero-residual cases in tests. If historical helper is excluded from the revised method, state this and avoid implying its outputs are current.

**Evidence required:** Isolated code change or clearly scoped replacement specification plus executable edge-case tests.  
**Work required:** code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R07 — Several sensitivity terms are not derivatives of the actual measured functional [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 5 Eqs. perimeter derivatives, conic covariance, curvature term; shoulder paragraph.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Because L is a vertical trace span, A_miss is an auxiliary model quantity rather than the removed XZ area.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The conic regression treats noisy design coordinates as fixed independent observations. Curvature uncertainty uses an auxiliary area and a different waist row; equivalent-circle propagation is not generally the derivative of a fitted elliptical perimeter. Shoulder receives a frontal-only budget, and depth capping assumes depth always maps to the fitted minor axis. The conditional alternative scale formula is also not the exact derivative of a complete arbitrary conic/morphology pipeline.

**Resolution criterion:** Derive sensitivity from the actual branch and output with the coordinate/fitting assumptions explicit, or remove unsupported components from the proposed method. Use perturbation reruns to test local derivatives and represent branch changes as nonsmooth. Preserve mathematically correct limited examples without presenting them as complete pipeline covariance.

**Evidence required:** Branch-consistent Jacobian or finite-difference experiments, orientation/depth counterexamples, corrected row/area handling or documented exclusion.  
**Work required:** math, code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R08 — Novelty is proposed, with no demonstrated advantage over the nearest prior art [MAJOR]

**Location:** Introduction and Related Work; Conclusion.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Its principal proposed adaptation couples a filtered profile interval to frontal run topology and a columnwise thickness bound.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The broad ingredients are established, and the current related-work discussion names previous two-view methods without a method-level comparison. A specific combination can be a contribution, but global priority or superiority is not established. A scientific journal needs either a new justified result or a demonstrated useful methodological advance.

**Resolution criterion:** Compare the exact profile/thickness mechanism and waist rule with the closest available two-view approaches, state which operation is new to this work and which is adopted, and restrict priority claims to what the search supports. Supply experiments under R03 for claims of advantage; a comparison table can support positioning but not performance.

**Evidence required:** Primary-source comparison and documented search scope/date, explicit contribution boundary, matched baseline/ablation results where superiority is claimed.  
**Work required:** data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R09 — Waist curvature is converted into sagittal section deletion without anatomical identification [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 3.3, Eq. waist displacement and taper.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The displacement uses half the circular sagitta

**Why this damages acceptance:** The small-curvature expansion is mathematically consistent. What is unproved is the inference from a frontal waist-width curve to how much volume should be removed in XZ. The factor one-half, 0.40 cap, 15 mm band, and taper are design choices, and deletion can worsen an already underestimated section. Tapering about global Z=0 also assumes meaningful centring.

**Resolution criterion:** Present this as a bounded heuristic with its exact geometric effect and applicability conditions; test no-op/monotonicity and off-centre profiles, analyze parameter sensitivity, and establish usefulness through matched waist references. Do not call it a physical correction or mathematical recovery theorem absent stronger evidence.

**Evidence required:** Analytic bounds or executable tests plus independent waist ablation; derivation/documentation of the taper origin and chosen constants.  
**Work required:** math, code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R10 — Column-thickness and profile interpolation assumptions lack validity limits [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 3.2 profile filtering and Eq. profile-guided occupancy.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** an approximately horizontal limb has a short vertical extent at its outer columns

**Why this damages acceptance:** Vertical run length is a reasonable thickness proxy for some horizontal limbs but can be arbitrarily larger for sloping/vertical limbs. A shared sagittal midpoint loses limb-depth placement; replacing rejected runs may violate silhouette consistency. Global width/centre medians and 51/101/61-pixel windows can also suppress real anatomy and vary physically with resolution.

**Resolution criterion:** State a bounded intended pose/domain and clarify that the reconstruction is prior-conditioned rather than uniquely recovered. Test horizontal, oblique and vertical limbs, multi-run rows, off-centre depth and missing/interpolated bands. Measure sensitivity to resampling/filter bandwidth. Human robustness remains an R02 requirement.

**Evidence required:** Reproducible synthetic/failure cases, parameter and resolution sensitivity, branch failure flags and observed/interpolated-band analysis.  
**Work required:** math, code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R11 — Capture assumptions have no quantitative operating limits [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 2.1 camera model and acquisition; Section 6.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The implementation estimates neither camera intrinsics nor residual yaw

**Why this damages acceptance:** Separate stature scales do not remove perspective variation, camera pitch/yaw, inter-view articulation, or clipping after alignment. The photos have no documented camera distance, focal information, orthogonality check or pose protocol. These are major sources of physical measurement bias.

**Resolution criterion:** Specify a reproducible capture protocol and exclusion checks; quantify acceptable distance/depth ratios and yaw/pose perturbations for the claimed domain. If inputs lack those records, report them as unavailable and avoid declaring robust accuracy under unspecified capture.

**Evidence required:** Camera/capture metadata or new controlled acquisition; synthetic/projective perturbation tests and repeat captures.  
**Work required:** math, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R12 — A detailed description is not yet a reproducible scientific release [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 4.3 retained outputs; entire implementation description.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Reproducibility therefore requires retaining the diagnostic log with image/model identifiers and numerical settings.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The paper gives many constants, but no accessible frozen implementation/version, locked environment, input list, exact run command, model hash or per-result manifest. The earlier local audit contains hashes, yet those are not a portable release. The historical requirements use open-ended dependencies, and logs omit structured advisory fields.

**Resolution criterion:** Supply a frozen research package or repository archive with versioned code, environment, model/source identifiers, run configurations, output schemas and synthetic data/tests. For private human inputs supply a justified access statement and enough metadata to trace reported outcomes. Clearly distinguish the historical snapshot, corrected reference code, and current application.

**Evidence required:** Packaged release/manifest/checksums, clean-environment reproduction log and documented data availability. A local reference harness can close computational reproducibility only.  
**Work required:** code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R13 — Pooled site-level summaries overstate the amount and comparability of evidence [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 6 Table 1 and pooled summary.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Across these ten comparisons, mean absolute error is 2.69 cm

**Why this damages acceptance:** Ten rows are only three participants, with missing sites and distinct shoulder/girth constructs. Midpoints replace reference ranges without their spread. MAE/MAPE across heterogeneous endpoints obscure site-specific behavior, and the study cannot estimate population agreement or precision.

**Resolution criterion:** Report corrected outcomes per participant and endpoint, preserve reference intervals/repeats, clearly describe missing observations, and avoid treating ten sites as independent subjects. Provide participant-level analysis and uncertainty on performance only with a suitable independent sample; do not imply Bland-Altman validity from merely citing its paper.

**Evidence required:** Raw reference readings/ranges, corrected case table, clustered/participant-level evaluation if adequate data exist.  
**Work required:** data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R14 — Development-sample tuning is not separated from evaluation [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 4.1 strong section opening and pilot; historical measurement_extraction.py lines 218 onward.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** verified against the sample photos: a small fixed radius left the fit ... overshot the true size substantially [source comment]

**Why this damages acceptance:** Historical source comments explicitly say rounding was checked against the sample photos. The same three developer-participants may therefore inform both parameter choice and reported results. The manuscript gives no tuning history or held-out boundary, so even corrected pilot error could be optimistic.

**Resolution criterion:** Disclose known sample-based development, label these subjects as development cases, freeze parameters before independent evaluation, and separate tuning from testing. Do not assert absence of tuning merely because the exact history is incomplete.

**Evidence required:** Contemporaneous tuning records where available; frozen configuration timestamp and held-out evaluation manifest.  
**Work required:** data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R15 — Authorship, participant governance and data statements are incomplete [MAJOR]

**Location:** Author line; Section 6; absent end matter.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Author details to be completed

**Why this damages acceptance:** Human photographs and body measurements are involved. Author-participation does not itself establish consent, publication permission, ethics exemption or review. The paper lacks affiliation/contact, actual consent/ethics status, privacy/data handling, funding and competing-interest declarations.

**Resolution criterion:** Authors supply accurate authorship/affiliation and applicable ethics approval/exemption or review status, informed consent and image-publication permissions if needed, data-access/privacy, funding and conflict statements. Never invent approval, consent or an exemption. An explicit 'not supplied' placeholder is honest but not submission-ready.

### R16 — Numerical stability and degeneracy handling are underspecified [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 4.1 Eq. fixed-constant fit; Section 3.2 polynomial filtering.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** (M^T M) phi = -M^T 1

**Why this damages acceptance:** Normal equations square the design condition number; coordinates in millimetres have mixed quadratic/linear scales. At least five hull vertices does not guarantee full rank, and an unconstrained conic may be invalid. The sign correction alone does not address ill-conditioned/noisy cases. The conceptual local-polynomial inverse is also not a recommendation to invert explicitly.

**Resolution criterion:** Use and document normalized coordinates with QR/SVD least squares and rank/conditioning checks in any corrected reference implementation, or justify equivalently stable handling. Test translated, rotated, high-aspect, noisy and rank-deficient cases; distinguish numerical verification from human validity. A solver change must be versioned and its effect reported.

**Evidence required:** Executable numerical tests, rank/condition/failure criteria, solver/version details and comparison with the historical normal-equation fit.  
**Work required:** math, code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R17 — No measured computational feasibility evidence [MAJOR]

**Location:** Sections 3.1 and 4.3 storage/memory statements.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The fine stage stores a dense array over this box.

**Why this damages acceptance:** A 2 mm whole-body dense grid plus full coordinate arrays, morphology and repeated meshing can be memory-intensive. Chunk size 64 and a byte-count formula do not establish runtime, peak memory, or failure behavior. The paper cannot support efficiency claims or practical deployment feasibility without measurements.

**Resolution criterion:** Report hardware, software, image/grid dimensions, elapsed stage/end-to-end time and measured peak memory for representative completed cases, including failure handling. If no benchmark is performed, remove any efficiency/deployment claim and explicitly bound computational evidence to verified synthetic sizes.

**Evidence required:** Benchmark script/logs with process memory and wall-time methodology; no extrapolated measurements presented as observed.  
**Work required:** code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R18 — Branch-dependent outputs can change the measurand without a machine-readable distinction [MAJOR]

**Location:** Sections 3.1, 4.2, 4.3 and result records.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The legacy fallback label can cover either breadth method

**Why this damages acceptance:** Chest can switch geometric source; shoulder fallback changes a modeled posterior path into projected breadth; 'inseam' denotes a hip-to-ankle proxy. A single value or legacy label cannot support reliable comparisons, and important advisory messages may be lost in logs.

**Resolution criterion:** Define distinct endpoint/method identifiers and serialize source branch, fallback reason, level, interpolation/capping/correction flags, units and unavailable sensitivity status. Do not pool shoulder breadth with path estimates. Demonstrate branch/fallback tests or explicitly exclude unresolved legacy outputs from the proposed method.

**Evidence required:** Versioned schema with examples and tests or explicit scoped exclusion of the uncorrected operational outputs.  
**Work required:** code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R19 — Failure/rejection behavior is not evaluated [MAJOR]

**Location:** Section 2.1 segmentation cleanup; Section 2.2 alignment; Section 3 component selection.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** The largest 26-connected volume component is retained.

**Why this damages acceptance:** Largest-component selection can delete a valid disconnected limb, pose thresholds do not ensure anatomical accuracy, and fixed-canvas warping can clip crown/feet before stature calibration. Rejecting failures without an attempted-input denominator biases performance, while same-image deterministic repetitions do not measure capture robustness.

**Resolution criterion:** Specify measurable rejection flags for clipping/missing landmarks/component loss, retain all attempted cases in a failure denominator, and evaluate both successful-output error and failure rate. Test disconnected masks, border clipping, pose failure and missing profile bands. Do not present pixel-identical reruns as accuracy evidence.

**Evidence required:** Failure-case tests and per-attempt audit; independent acquisition failure rates under R02.  
**Work required:** code, data, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R20 — Display geometry may be mistaken for the geometry used to report measurements [MINOR]

**Location:** Section 4.3 display mesh and export.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** A rounded copy of the profile-guided volume is used for display.

**Why this damages acceptance:** The text correctly separates branches, but readers receive no compact source-to-output mapping or reproducible example showing that mesh smoothing/export does not define the quoted girths. Large junction closings and mesh smoothing can alter appearance materially.

**Resolution criterion:** Give a concise branch-to-output mapping or concrete worked example, preserve the statement that display mesh is not validated anatomical geometry, and test coordinate/unit/orientation transformations on a known object. Do not add a mesh accuracy claim without appropriate surface reference.

**Evidence required:** Coordinate/units regression example and an unambiguous branch mapping; visual example if available.  
**Work required:** code, prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.

### R21 — The manuscript reads as an implementation audit without a resolved research result [MINOR]

**Location:** Abstract, Introduction and Conclusion; overall editorial presentation.  
**Evidence/excerpt:** Its effect on measurement accuracy remains to be established through corrected, independently collected experiments.

**Why this damages acceptance:** Repeated admissions about a historical bug and future validation overwhelm the central scientific question. These statements are true and necessary; their negative tone is not the underlying acceptance problem. A 20-reference quota and a 12-page cap do not compensate for missing evidence.

**Resolution criterion:** After substantive work, lead the abstract with the actual verified result and its domain; consolidate limitations once and place historical defect details in a traceable supplement where the main claim remains truthful. Keep all necessary disclosures and preserve >=20 relevant references in first-citation order and <=12 pages including references. No cosmetic deletion can close R01-R19.

**Evidence required:** Revised full manuscript with compilation/page/citation audit, linked resolution table and honest bounded claims.  
**Work required:** prose.  
**Round-1 status:** OPEN.
