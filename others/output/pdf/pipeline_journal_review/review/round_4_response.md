## Evidence and its limits

`candidate_results_v3.json` contains **598 unique attempts**, including direct known raster sections and corresponding two-axis projected occupancy. Disjoint development/test configurations cover ellipse/circle, superellipse, box/rounded box, asymmetry and concavity with varied orientation, resolution and smooth boundary noise. Every attempted candidate and failure is retained. There is one deterministic noise field per configuration, not a statistical repeatability or coverage study. Continuous section/convex references and noiseless raster references are separate; reference-boundary refinement changes perimeter by at most 0.00138 mm.

For noiseless direct ellipse/circle sections, constrained-fit MAE is 9.37/4.24/2.54 mm at grids 4/2/1 mm. The .9-opening comparator gives 35.32/34.04/39.28 mm. The projected experiments are materially different: the vertex-weighted fit is unavailable for all 243 projected test hulls (four vertices). Even a valid finite ellipse can have a large error on model-mismatched direct sections. Family-specific results are in the main candidate table and `candidate_analysis_v3.json`; no pooled winner is selected. This demonstrates removal of forced section distortion and clarifies model dependence, without claiming that an unrounded box is a body ellipse.

`candidate_diagnostics_v3.py` adds centre/span ratios, conditioning, support information and gradient-normalized algebraic residuals without changing frozen estimates or selecting a model. Constrained conditioning is `s1/s5` of the six-column normalized design; the fixed-constant condition is the full five-column design condition. These are distinct diagnostics. Residual support also differs for the post-opening comparator, so a small residual on that altered support is not a certificate of preserved input geometry. Spreads including the legacy comparator are labelled separately from current-only model spreads. Neither is a physical confidence interval.

`waist_policy_v3.py` makes deletion explicitly optional. A local positive-curvature guard rejects negative curvature, short support and endpoint-only support; flat support yields zero. Configuration, source branch and actual area/perimeter changes accompany a distinct hypothesis record. `waist_policy_results_v3.json` contains five trace cases, **32 actual inherited-taper reruns** across four section families, displacements 0/5/10/20 mm and sagittal offsets 0/40 mm, plus invalid-configuration tests. Base occupancy is unchanged; all tested valid hypotheses are deletion-only. A centred box at d=20 mm loses 7988 voxels while its convex perimeter is unchanged; shifting the sagittal coordinates by 40 mm changes deletion to 8694 voxels and perimeter by -36.69 mm. This supports the nonoverwriting policy and shows why area/volume change is not a girth effect. It does not establish anatomical benefit.

`../integrated/profile_ablation.py` compares unfiltered widest-run, filtered/no-cap and filtered/vertical-column-cap slices on a common physical grid. The cap uses the connected **vertical** frontal run per X at the reported Y. All variants share torso clipping ×1.15. It records activation before/after clipping, changed voxels and paired candidate perimeter changes. A synthetic invariant has 130 active outer-arm columns before clipping but zero afterward; the torso level is unchanged. Thus whole-volume IoU improvements are not automatically improvements in reported anthropometry. Root integrates this helper for actual inputs.

## Completed integrated and external evidence

The final isolated driver produces candidate sets on two of three development RGB pairs and rejects one at the unchanged profile-mask border-contact criterion. The two completed attempts take 8.18/6.34 seconds with observed process peak working sets 2.22/1.80 GB. These are illustrative concurrent-run observations, not a controlled deployment benchmark. Eight separately injected control-path tests pass; their mocked nature is explicit. Actual private RGB inputs and the public supplied-silhouette entry are not conflated.

The public BodyM benchmark uses all **87 Test-A metadata subjects**, each with its lexicographically first frontal/profile pair. Code/configuration and selection rule were frozen before reading this evaluation's reference labels. Because the project had an older BodyM evaluation script, this is labelled **retrospective external evaluation**, not an untouched holdout. Primary source Ruiz et al. (2022), the official project and registry establish that masks come from real photographs in A-pose/tight clothing and labels are scan/registered-mesh-derived vertex-path measurements. The post-perception runner uses supplied height and fixed stature-fraction levels; these labels are prediction targets rather than matched-plane tape ground truth.

All 87 acquisitions succeed. The fixed input-border rule rejects 30; 57 produce candidate sets. The main table displays conventional/profile constrained and classical width/depth MAE/RMSE with availability out of 87 for every site. Full six-candidate outcomes, signed error and paired counts are in `../integrated/bodym_metrics.json`, `.csv` and `bodym_predictions.csv`. Profile constrained chest/waist/hip MAEs are 321.50/96.23/49.54 mm, while width/depth MAEs are 134.33/54.12/113.20 mm. These heterogeneous endpoints are not pooled into a headline participant estimate.

On common subjects, constrained profile-minus-conventional absolute-error changes are -19.10 mm chest (n=57), +1.38 mm waist (n=54), -5.12 mm hip (n=56). A common-grid comparison isolates the cap from filtering: constrained errors change -12.54/-0.98/-2.34 mm at chest/waist/hip, respectively. At chest 29 improve and 28 remain unchanged. Occupancy changes in 45/57, 4/57 and 18/57 sections; width/depth predictions are unchanged at every site because the extents remain unchanged. The common ablation grid can have a different sagittal phase from the complete profile-volume grid, and those records are kept distinct. This supplies a bounded estimator-dependent measurement result without asserting universal superiority or matched physical accuracy.
