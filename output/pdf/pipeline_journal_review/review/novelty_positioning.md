Date: 2026-10-21. This is a bounded method comparison, not a systematic review or a patent/priority clearance. Search terms included the exact Aslam/Ji titles, `anthropometry two silhouettes visual hull depth arms profile thickness reconstruction`, and `anthropometry silhouette column thickness body reconstruction`. Searches and the existing bibliography were followed to accessible author or publisher material. The search cannot establish that an operation is absent from all prior work.

The proposed operation is the combination of a robust, interpolated profile interval with frontal connected vertical-run thickness at each lateral column, with source branches specified before section fitting. The waist displacement is described as a bounded shape heuristic. The supplied synthetic experiments test favorable mechanisms and show adverse cases. They do not establish physical anthropometric utility or superiority over the above systems. The revised paper therefore avoids global priority, first-of-kind and human-accuracy advantage claims. R08 remains partly unresolved: a closer, broader review and independently evaluated advantage are still needed for an evaluated journal-method claim.
