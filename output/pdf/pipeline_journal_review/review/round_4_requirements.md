## Priority 1: repair the measurement-prior design (R03, R09)

The existing 0.9 opening causes a known-ellipse error of -44.918 mm at 2 mm spacing, versus -0.974 mm with opening bypassed. The source-based experiment establishes a material design problem, separate from the corrected conic sign. Do not choose a different fraction merely to improve those same 32 examples.

The rectifier's proposed research redesign is worth implementing and evaluating: remove unconditional section opening from the base research measurement path; retain the unrounded-occupancy convex perimeter as a **geometric diagnostic**; report model-conditional ellipse estimates in separate fields. Evaluate the source-corrected unrounded fixed-constant fit, an attributed ellipse-specific fit, width/depth ellipse approximation, unrounded convex-polygon perimeter and the legacy 0.9-opening path as distinct candidates. A model estimate and a geometric diagnostic must not silently substitute for one another.

The word “prior-independent” would be inaccurate for the resulting anatomy: visual-hull/profile reconstruction already imposes assumptions. The precise narrower distinction is “without additional section-rounding or waist-deletion conditioning.” An unrounded rectangular visual hull is not necessarily a true body section. Likewise, a fitted ellipse on a non-ellipse is a conditional model, even if the algebra is stable.

Freeze algorithms, candidate-selection policy, settings, metrics and declared development/test shape families before evaluating the new test set. Include ellipses/circles, superellipses, boxes/rounded boxes, asymmetry and concavity; vary orientation, shape aspect, grid spacing and boundary noise. Retain every attempt, invalid conic and model disagreement. Separate analytic truth from rasterized truth where discretization matters. A contour-preserving estimate should show appropriate convergence with grid refinement; ellipse recovery should preserve known ellipses in the noiseless limit and disclose noise/partial-contour failures. Report performance by family rather than hiding model failure in a pooled average. A synthetic held-out family is evidence about synthetic generalization only.

No runtime estimator may use the unavailable true family or true perimeter to choose its output. If a candidate is selected after development experiments, freeze that decision before held-out evaluation and state the selection rule and development history. It is also legitimate to report competing model estimates without selecting a winner; their intended meanings must be clear.

Two comparison controls are required. First, use a common contour sampling/weighting control for fixed-constant versus ellipse-specific solver comparisons; if the historical hull-vertex weighting is retained, report it separately from uniform arc sampling. Second, distinguish direct-rasterized true-section experiments from projected/reconstructed-section experiments. An ellipse intersected from two orthogonal silhouettes can become a rectangular section: recovering an ellipse from its true contour does not demonstrate girth recovery from that reconstructed box. Both stages should be evaluated and labelled, with the actual pipeline geometry exercised by integration tests.

For the waist rule, make the base output unadjusted and treat deletion as a separately configured hypothesis unless evidence supports a stronger role. Optional adjusted output must have a distinct identifier and record the displacement/configuration, source branch, actual area/perimeter changes and applicability/admissibility flags. It must not silently lower the primary value. Test zero/no-op, bounded deletion, positive/negative curvature cases, short support, off-centre sagittal support and sensitivity across section families. Geometric monotonicity and deletion bounds do not establish anatomical correctness. If a physically corrected waist remains the claim, matched physical evidence is still required.

**Potential closure:** robustly demonstrated removal of forced shape distortion, accurate field meanings and prespecified broad computational comparisons can close the computational redesign parts of R03/R09. Demonstrated anatomical usefulness remains dependent on R02/R04 and cannot be supplied by generated shapes alone.

## Priority 3: distinguish usable independent data from merely accessible files (R02, R04, R10, R11, R14)

Archived photographs, masks and tape values can support corrected development reruns if their mapping/provenance is established. They do not become an independent held-out evaluation because the current agent sees them for the first time. Source comments show development-photo checking. Determine which observations informed tuning, who collected each reference, and whether a genuinely independent frozen evaluation boundary can be established. Do not infer independence or documented procedure from filenames.

Three evidence classes should remain distinct:

| Evidence class | What it can establish | What it cannot establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Independently acquired real RGB observations with documented matched physical/reference measurements | End-to-end physical accuracy, capture/failure behavior and repeatability within the actual study domain | Population generalization beyond the justified sampling/measurement design |
| Silhouettes segmented from independently captured real photographs, with documented scan/reference labels | Genuine human post-perception geometry/measurement performance for matched endpoints | Performance of the paper's RGB segmentation/pose stage or tape agreement when labels are scan-derived |
| Measured real-body scans/meshes with rendered silhouettes or virtual cameras | Reconstruction and section-model behavior on real body geometry under controlled synthetic capture | Real photographic acquisition/perception validity or tape agreement unless those are separately provided |
| Fully generated shapes, synthetic avatars or analytical primitives | Numerical correctness, controlled geometry mechanisms, counterexamples and conditional robustness | Independent human anthropometric agreement or ethical status of a real-participant study |

A public dataset may be valuable if access/licensing and reference definitions support the intended use. Inspect primary dataset documentation and actual downloadable contents before promising an evaluation. Use a reference endpoint that is genuinely available: a mesh-plane perimeter is not automatically a tape circumference, and a maximum-girth benchmark is not automatically the paper's prescribed level. If dataset terms, restricted access or absent labels prevent the required experiment, record that fact rather than inventing labels or treating virtual-camera experiments as real capture.

The reviewer independently checked the [BodyM primary paper](https://adversarialbodysim.github.io/files/paper.pdf), Sections 3.2 and 4: its released silhouettes come from segmentation of actual photographs, labels follow paths on registered scans, and original RGB is withheld. This is genuine independent human post-perception evidence, rather than virtual-camera evidence. An actual frozen, endpoint-aligned evaluation could therefore substantively resolve a bounded downstream-geometry validation claim under R02; it would not validate the paper's MediaPipe stage. The registered-mesh path definitions must be matched rather than equated automatically with the paper's proportional plane levels. Access to a dataset alone is not an evaluation.

There are two legitimate evaluation questions. A claim of directly measuring a specified section/path needs that section/path matched. Alternatively, a fixed image-plane/model quantity may be declared an **estimator of a dataset-defined anthropometric target**. In that predictive question, differences of level/model are part of the observed target error and must remain in the results; an exactly corresponding image plane is not a prerequisite. The target definition and per-subject pairing must be explicit, no scan label may be called a tape reference, and prediction error cannot be interpreted as isolated reconstruction/ellipse error. This distinction permits a useful benchmark without inventing anatomical correspondence or weakening the requirement that compared target quantities be defined.

The author has confirmed **physical tape measurement on volunteers, upright posture and repeated measurements**. This settles the instrument, participation, broad posture and occurrence of repeats. R04 still needs actual anatomical levels/endpoints, available repeated readings or summaries/ranges and per-reference linkage, and the recorded details of clothing/respiration/tape procedure where relevant. Named operators and identifiable locations are not required; anonymous session/procedure information may suffice. Ask for facts that exist; do not demand the authors reconstruct a procedure they did not record. If unknown, retain that limitation and arrange a suitable prospective evaluation rather than writing invented details.

Keep endpoint types separate: prescribed chest-level, waist-proxy and hip-landmark girths; posterior shoulder path versus projected shoulder breadth; vertical hip-to-ankle proxy versus inseam. Independent evaluation requires matched endpoint definitions, justified participant-level design and all attempted captures. Repeated body sites do not create independent participants. Useful agreement criteria must come from intended use/precision goals or an explicitly justified study question, not an arbitrary acceptance threshold invented by the reviewer.

**Potential closure:** an appropriate documented independent dataset and actual integrated evaluation can resolve R02 for a bounded stated domain. Real-body geometry rendered to images supplies narrower evidence and can only close issues matching that narrower claim. No prespecified numerical sample count is invented here; the design and precision target require justification.
