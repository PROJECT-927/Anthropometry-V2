Body Measurement from Two Photos

Choose front and side photos and enter your height. Photos are processed on this device; only silhouettes and pose landmarks are sent for measurement.

Height is the only scale reference — every measurement is calibrated from it, so an inaccurate height rescales all results proportionally.
Pose matters: stand straight, arms hanging down but angled out roughly 15–20° so there is a visible gap between arm and torso — and keep that same angle in both photos. Arms flush against the body, or held straight out, both degrade the measurements. The browser processes the photos locally and uploads only the derived black-and-white masks and pose points ({{ max_upload_mb }}MB request limit).
