Body Measurement from Two Photos

Upload a front and side photo, enter your real height, and the pipeline reconstructs a 3D hull to measure from.

Height is the only scale reference — every measurement is calibrated from it, so an inaccurate height rescales all results proportionally.
Pose matters: stand straight, arms hanging down but angled out roughly 15–20° so there is a visible gap between arm and torso — and keep that same angle in both photos. Arms flush against the body, or held straight out, both degrade the measurements. Max {{ max_upload_mb }}MB total.
